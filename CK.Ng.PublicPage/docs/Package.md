The public page of an Angular application: the `PublicPageComponent` is the routed component of the application root
(the `""` route) and has routes, so that the packages route their public pages under it. Its `PublicPage` component
(`ck-public-page`) is a `<router-outlet />` that the packages change with their transformers.
See the [README](https://github.com/signature-opensource/CK-TS-Angular/blob/stable/CK.Ng.PublicPage/README.md).
